My second claim regards the answer. **Can we know the answer to this most important question? Yes — we *can* know.**

## “But can anyone really *know*?”

Some say there isn’t enough evidence. Others say that a question this large — this life-shaping — would require immense, even overwhelming evidence.

I agree, in part. If you are going to answer the most important question, you should have evidence, and it should be evidence that is *accessible to you*.

There is evidence. There is far too much to state here — which is exactly why I am confident in this claim.

## Where the evidence is found

The evidence comes from several disciplines: cosmology, physics, philosophy, the nature of morality and of the mind. Each is worth exploring.

But the most striking evidence is **historical**. It concerns a real person, in a real place, at a known time — Jesus of Nazareth — and what happened after He was executed. That is a claim history can investigate.

<blockquote>
How serious you are about the question will shape how serious you are about examining the evidence.
</blockquote>
