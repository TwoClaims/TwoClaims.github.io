The Gospels are the best place to meet Jesus in his own words. *The Chosen* is a good place to *see* him. It's a historical drama about his life, told through the people who knew him: fishermen, a tax collector, a woman with a troubled past, and religious leaders trying to work out who he was.

## Why I recommend it

This site argues that Jesus was a real person in real history. *The Chosen* makes that history feel close. The people around Jesus aren't figures in a stained-glass window. They're ordinary people with jobs, families, doubts and arguments, meeting someone they can't explain. These are the same people who later said they saw him alive.

<Aside type="note" title="A drama, not a documentary">
The series stays close to the Gospels, but it fills in backstories, conversations and characters that the Gospels don't record. Watch it alongside the Gospels themselves, and let the text have the final word.
</Aside>
