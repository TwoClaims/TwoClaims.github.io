Every claim should be challenged. Here are some of the objections most often raised, with places to find thoughtful answers.

## “Why would a good God send anyone to hell?”

Imagine designing a country with free healthcare, great schools, and honest leaders — but no justice system. Anyone could steal, assault, or murder without consequence. Would it still be a *good* country?

Goodness and justice go hand in hand. God is loving, merciful, and kind — and also perfectly just. At the cross, justice and mercy meet: Jesus took the penalty that sin deserves, so that all who repent and trust in Him can be forgiven.

> God is equally loving and just.
