If the evidence leads where we have claimed — that God exists, and that He has made Himself known in Jesus — then the most important question has a personal follow-up:

<p class="tc-question">What will you do with Jesus?</p>

## The good news

- **God is real, good, and just.** He made you, and you matter to Him.
- **We have all fallen short.** Each of us has done wrong, and wrong deserves justice.
- **Jesus took our place.** He died for sin and rose again, defeating death.
- **Forgiveness is offered freely.** Not earned, but received — by turning from sin and trusting in Him.

## Next steps

Read one of the Gospels for yourself — the Gospel of John is a good place to start. Talk to a Christian you trust. Ask God honestly, *“If you are there, show me.”*

<Aside type="note" title="Still unsure?">
That's fine. Keep asking. Keep examining. Don't stop until you are satisfied.
</Aside>
