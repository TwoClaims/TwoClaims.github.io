Questions. We all ask them. The answers often raise more questions. This site makes **two claims** about one question and its answer — and invites you to test them.

<p class="tc-question">Is there a God?</p>

1. **The Question.** The most important question for every human being is, “Is there a God?”
2. **The Answer.** There is more than sufficient evidence to answer it — and the most striking evidence is historical, centred on Jesus of Nazareth.

Notice what the second claim does *not* say. It does not begin with “Yes, there is a God.” It says that you **can** arrive at an answer, because the evidence is there to be examined.

## How to use this site

This site is deliberately short. It raises the question, lays out the shape of the evidence, and then points you to people who have studied each area far more deeply. Follow the trail as far as your questions take you.
