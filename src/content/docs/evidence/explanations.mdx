Historians test a theory by asking how much of the evidence it explains — and how much it has to explain *away*. Here are the most common explanations for what happened after Jesus died, set against the facts.

<ExplanationMatrix />

## The explanations

### Resurrection
Jesus actually rose from the dead, as His followers claimed.

### Swoon
Jesus did not really die on the cross; He revived in the tomb.

### Conspiracy
The disciples stole the body and invented the story.

### Hallucination
The disciples experienced grief-induced visions.

### Legend
The story grew over time and the early facts were lost.

### Wrong tomb
The women and disciples went to the wrong tomb and found it empty.

### Unknown
“Something happened, but we can’t say what.” This is honest — but it is not an explanation.
