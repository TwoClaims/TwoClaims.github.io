Each line of evidence below is summarised in a sentence or two, with a pointer to people who have done the deep work. None of them stands alone (except, perhaps, the [historical argument](#history)); together they form a cumulative and solid case.

## Cosmology

The universe had a beginning. Whatever begins to exist has a cause. What caused the universe?

<ResourceList ids={['reasonable-faith']} />

## Fine-tuning

The physical constants of the universe fall within extraordinarily narrow ranges that permit life. Chance, necessity, or design?

## Morality

We all live as though some things are *really* wrong — not just unpopular. Where does a moral law come from, if not from a moral lawgiver?

## Mind

Consciousness, reason, and our trust in our own thinking are difficult to explain as by-products of unguided matter.

## History

The most striking evidence of all — because it can be investigated like any other event in the past.
