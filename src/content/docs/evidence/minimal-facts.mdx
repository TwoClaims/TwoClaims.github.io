> The Minimal Facts approach identifies a core set of historically attested events surrounding Jesus’ death and resurrection that are widely accepted by scholars, including skeptics, to build a historical case for the resurrection.

## The facts

1. **Jesus died by crucifixion.**
2. **His disciples sincerely believed He rose and appeared to them.**
3. **Paul, a persecutor of the church, was suddenly converted.**
4. **James, Jesus’ skeptical brother, was suddenly converted.**
5. **The tomb was empty.** *(Widely, though not as universally, accepted.)*

## What makes these facts “minimal”?

Each is supported by multiple, early, independent sources — and each is granted by the large majority of scholars who study the period, whatever their own beliefs.
