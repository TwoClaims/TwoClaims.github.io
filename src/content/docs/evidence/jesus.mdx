Most arguments for God ask you to reason from the world around you. The historical evidence is different: it asks you to look at a specific event and ask what best explains it.

Jesus of Nazareth claimed to speak and act with the authority of God. He was publicly executed under Roman authority. Within weeks, His followers were proclaiming — at great personal cost — that He had been raised from the dead.

**If that happened, the question “Is there a God?” has been answered.** If it did not, Christianity collapses; its own earliest writers said so (1 Corinthians 15:14–17).

That makes it a claim worth investigating carefully.
