# Analogies in Evangelism

I just watched Ray Comfort's video, [my worst interviews ever](https://youtu.be/6JFt9b9TPw0?si=eYtlIV0RWIwt39H9). In reading the comments, I saw one by [@JaiyhanaTubbs-z9r](https://www.youtube.com/@JaiyhanaTubbs-z9r). Here is it (with minor formatting/typo fixes):

> I'm 16 and one day, while I was thinking about how to answer the question, "Why would a good God send anyone to hell?" the Holy Spirit brought an analogy to my mind that I've used many times when sharing the gospel.
>
> I begin by asking, "If you could create your own country, what attributes would make it good?" Most people answer with things like free healthcare, a strong education system, no homelessness, honest leaders, and other positive qualities. Then I ask another question: "What if your country had all of those things but no justice system? Anyone could murder and get away with it. Steal and get away with it. Assault, rob, or ignore every law without consequences. Would your country still be good?"
> 
> Most people answer, "No."
> 
> That's the point.
> 
> Goodness and justice go hand in hand. A nation can have many wonderful qualities, but if evil is never confronted and wrongdoing is never judged, it cannot truly be called good.
> 
> The same principle applies to God. We often think of Him as loving, merciful, gracious, and kind—and He is. But God is also perfectly just. If He simply overlooked sin and never judged evil, He would no longer be perfectly good.
> 
> The Bible says that "the wages of sin is death" (Romans 6:23). God doesn't punish sin because He lacks love; He judges sin because He is perfectly holy and perfectly just. Yet, in His love, He made a way for justice and mercy to meet together. Jesus Christ took the punishment that sinners deserve by dying on the cross and rising again, so that everyone who repents and trusts in Him can be forgiven while God's justice is fully satisfied.
> 
> The cross is where we see both the justice of God and the love of God displayed perfectly.
> 
> I began this by staring my age so that all reading, whether young or old, would be encouraged to know that they can spread the gospel NOW. Whether at school, work, or a strange place - God has equipped those who are born again to share His good news! Dive into His word and study how you can reach different people. For example, "how to witness to Mormons". This is important because although the gospel never changes, we should learn how to approach and respond to different things with the Holy Spirit's help!! Repent, put your faith in God, and share the good news!! 

> God is equally loving and just!
